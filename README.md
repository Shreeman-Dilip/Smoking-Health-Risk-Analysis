# Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

The **Smoking Health Risk Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI report designed to analyze the impact of smoking habits on patient health and identify key health risk factors. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of smoking behavior, demographic patterns, organ health status, and associated medical risks through intuitive visualizations.

## Objectives

* Analyze smoking patterns across different patient groups.
* Identify relationships between smoking habits and health conditions.
* Evaluate the influence of risk factors such as hypertension, cholesterol, BMI, family history, and alcohol consumption.
* Support data-driven healthcare insights through interactive reporting.

## Key Features

* **Patient Overview:** Displays total patient count along with average age and BMI metrics.
* **Smoking Status Analysis:** Segments patients into Never, Current, and Former smokers for comparative analysis.
* **Smoking Duration & Daily Intake:** Examines years of smoking and cigarettes consumed per day across age groups.
* **Gender-Based Insights:** Visualizes smoking patterns among male and female patients.
* **Organ Health Monitoring:** Interactive organ selection (Heart, Kidneys, Liver, Lungs, and Human Body) to explore health impacts.
* **Cholesterol & Hypertension Risk Analysis:** Highlights risk distribution across different age groups.
* **Health Status Assessment:** Allows users to analyze Healthy versus Damaged patient conditions.

## Insights Generated

This dashboard helps uncover trends in smoking behavior and their potential impact on patient health, enabling better understanding of risk profiles and supporting informed healthcare decision-making.
